# Project Report & Methodology Notes

## 1. Problem Statement
Disaster events like flash floods and earthquakes cause sudden disruption to road networks and human safety. Existing emergency platforms often lack dynamic routing that adapts to real-time evolving hazard zones while balancing shelter capacities.

## 2. Objective
To build an intelligent, integrated decision-support platform that:
- Classifies flood and earthquake hazard risk levels using machine learning.
- Generates early warning signals when critical thresholds are exceeded.
- Computes dynamic, safe evacuation corridors by penalizing or eliminating routes intersecting hazard zones.
- Directs affected populations to nearest viable safe shelters.

## 3. Disasters in Scope
- **Flood:** Water level thresholds, rainfall volume, and risk level classification.
- **Earthquake:** Magnitude, focal depth, ground acceleration, and seismic severity classification.
